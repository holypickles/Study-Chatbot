# 🤖 Study-Chatbot - Your AI Study Companion

## 🌟 Overview
Study-Chatbot is your personal AI study assistant. With this tool, you can easily upload PDFs, engage in conversations with your study materials, and create practice exams complete with answer keys. It utilizes Streamlit for an intuitive interface and is powered by OpenAI GPT-4, combined with RAG (ChromaDB) for smart document retrieval.

## 💻 System Requirements
- **Operating System:** Windows 10 or later, macOS Big Sur or later, or any Linux distribution.
- **Memory:** At least 4 GB RAM.
- **Storage:** Minimum 200 MB of free disk space.
- **Internet Connection:** Required for file upload and AI processing.

## 🎯 Key Features
- **PDF Upload:** Easily upload your study materials in PDF format.
- **Intelligent Chat:** Engage in a conversation with your documents to clarify concepts and definitions.
- **Practice Exams:** Generate custom practice exams from your materials and get instant feedback.
- **Answer Keys:** After generating exams, receive answer keys to help you study effectively.
